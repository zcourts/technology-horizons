First Technology Horizons meeting

An open, free meeting for the technology community. Talks may be in Polish or English.

  • Audience: students, professionals, researchers, companies and people interested in technology.
  • Format: practical talks, questions, discussion and networking.
  • Venue: Faculty of Computer Science, Opole University of Technology.

How can you get involved?

Technology Horizons should grow with the community. The best topics often come from participants.

  1. Suggest a topic

    Tell us what you want to learn about: AI, architecture, cybersecurity, data, cloud, product or something niche.

  2. Offer a talk

    You can present a project, technical experience, case study, research or lessons from practice.

  3. Help build the community

    Share the event, invite people from the industry, recommend a speaker or suggest a partnership.
