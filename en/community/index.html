Community

An open community for people curious about technology

Technology Horizons is being built as a place for knowledge exchange between the university, business and practitioners. We welcome students, alumni, researchers, professionals, companies, founders and people just starting their path in technology.

Who is Technology Horizons for?

✦

Students and alumni

Access to practice, projects, career paths and people from the industry.

◉

Professionals and companies

A place to share knowledge, meet talent and build relationships.

⌬

Researchers and innovators

Space to show research, prototypes and applied uses of technology.

Community rules

Respect

We discuss constructively. Personal attacks, harassment and discrimination are not accepted.

Relevance

Posts should relate to technology, research, education, business, events or professional development.

Knowledge, not spam

Promotion is acceptable when it provides real value. We avoid mass advertising and purely sales-led content.

Polish and English

Posts, comments and talks may be in Polish or English. Both languages are welcome.

Confidentiality

Do not share confidential company, university, client, student or personal information without permission.

Relationship building

Help others find knowledge, contacts, speakers, projects and opportunities to collaborate.

University collaboration

The initiative is supported by the Faculty of Computer Science, Opole University of Technology. The goal is to create a lasting bridge between education, technology practice and the regional innovation ecosystem.

What can you do?

  • suggest a meeting topic
  • submit your own talk
  • recommend a speaker or company
  • share the event in your network
  • help as a community partner
