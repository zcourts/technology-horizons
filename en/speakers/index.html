Speakers

Share knowledge, a project or practical experience

We welcome speakers from universities, companies, startups, product teams, research labs and technology communities. You do not need to be a professional conference speaker — a clear story and value for participants matter most.

View events

What kind of talks are we looking for?

We are looking for talks that help people understand how technology is used in practice: what works, what fails, what is worth learning and which technical decisions have consequences.

Formats

Lightning talk

A short presentation or demo. Good for students, side projects and first-time speakers.

25–35 minute talk

The main meeting format: a practical topic, context, examples and takeaways.

Case study or demo

A walkthrough of a system, tool, process, implementation, failure or project lesson.

Panel or workshop

A format for topics that benefit from several perspectives or more active participant involvement.

Talk language

You can speak in Polish or English. The language will be clearly marked in the event programme so participants know what to expect.

Programme principles

Educational value

The best talks teach, show decision-making or inspire people to act.

No sales pitch

You may show a company or product in context, but the core content should be substantive.

Openness

We welcome practitioners, researchers, students, technical leaders and people building interesting things.
